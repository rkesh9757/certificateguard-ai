    Process

    Four steps, in order

    1. 01

      Submit

      Upload a certificate image or enter its printed details by hand.

    2. 02

      Extract

      On-device OCR reads the candidate name, institution, certificate ID, date and course from the image.

    3. 03

      Cross-check

      Every field is checked for format, plausibility, and a match against known verification records.

    4. 04

      Verdict

      A verdict — Likely Genuine, Suspicious, or Likely Fake — is issued with a confidence score and the full evidence log.

    CertificateGuard AI

    A prototype built to explore automated, evidence-backed certificate screening. All OCR runs locally in your browser — no file is uploaded to a server. Verification records used here are a small demo set, not a live registry.
